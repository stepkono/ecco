What happened

Every 5 s the post decides: quiet, recognized or unsure. Unsure moments in a row are collected into one recording. When it ends, it is saved and then sent to the server, where it appears on the website under Newest. If it is the same sound as an earlier recording, it is only counted as a repeat and not sent again.
