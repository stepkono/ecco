# ecco: voice script for the 1:30 pitch video

About 175 words. That fits 90 seconds at a calm pace, with short pauses. Clip names refer to `footage/`.

| Time | Voice | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Forests are full of voices. Frogs, birds, insects. Some of them are disappearing, and almost nobody is listening. | Spiral B-roll, slow turn (`14-spiral-orbit-resolutions-clean.mp4`) |
| 0:10 to 0:24 | Meet ecco. Small listening posts, built on the Arduino UNO Q, listen in the forest day and night. The AI runs right on the board. It decides in milliseconds, without the cloud. And human voices never leave it. | Your own shot of the board with the camera mic. Then the board console with "Listening to the mic" |
| 0:24 to 0:34 | When a post is not sure, it asks for help. It sends only that short moment. Nothing else. | Welcome screen, Newest with the live recordings (`01-welcome-screen-categories-and-map.mp4`, then `11-live-recording-from-the-board-parts.mp4`) |
| 0:34 to 0:56 | On ecco, anyone can help. The sound becomes a landscape you can explore. Press play and follow the call. Paint it with the brush. Echo finds every repeat for you. Then give it a name. | Reveal (`02`), playback (`03`), brush (`05`), Echo (`06`) |
| 0:56 to 1:08 | One label is enough. Within seconds, the listening post learns the new sound. From then on it recognizes it on its own, in every recording that follows. | Labeling card with Send label, then the board console with "Learned" and "Recognized" |
| 1:08 to 1:18 | Every sound you solve becomes a stamp in your archive. Labeling stops being a chore. It becomes discovery. | Archive with sound stamps, then themes (`10-view-lab-themes.mp4`) |
| 1:18 to 1:30 | A community that teaches the forest to listen. Owned by people, not by big tech. ecco. Help the forest listen. | Outro (`12-outro-junction-2026.mp4`), then the ecco logo |

## Notes for recording

- **Pace:** slow and calm. Pause briefly after "Meet ecco", after "Nothing else" and before the last line.
- **Missing footage:** there is no clip yet of the real board, the "Send label" moment, the archive and the console lines "Learned" and "Recognized". Film the board with a phone, and record the screen once for the other three.
- **Facts behind the claims:**
  - "in milliseconds": about 9 ms per sound window on the board
  - "within seconds": 1.4 to 2.0 s from label to board
  - "voices never leave it": the board drops speech by default
  - "only that short moment": only unsure sounds are sent, at most 15 s each
